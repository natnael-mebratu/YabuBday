October 26, 2026

Happy 20th, Yabu.

You're 20 now, and this year I'm the one making you a card. I'm following in his footsteps.

So get comfortable. Read this slowly. Every word is for you.

chapter one

Look at you now.

Remember who you were a few years ago? I do. You decided you were going to change, and then you actually did it. Every day, even when nobody was watching.

That muscle isn't just muscle. It's proof that when you decide something, it happens. Never forget you're capable of that.

chapter two

That brain of yours.

You went from near the bottom of your class to the top 15. Do you understand how rare that is?

You can read something for 20 minutes and know it by heart. I've watched you do it, and I'm still not over it. You are so damn smart, Yabu.

So here's my one ask: push yourself until you hit your limit. I don't think you've found it yet. I don't think it's anywhere close.

chapter three

The way you built a life.

You got kicked out of a house. You lived in a shelter. And somehow, through all of it, you kept your life clean, organized and steady. That alone would've broken a lot of people.

But you didn't stop there. You found amazing friends. You built a circle. You found a girlfriend. The kid who used to struggle to make friends now makes them everywhere he goes, like it's nothing.

Honestly? I'm jealous. Teach me how you do it. 😂

chapter four

The purest heart I know.

Tap the pictures.

You have the purest heart of anyone I've ever known. The same face in those pictures is the same heart you carry today. Good, gentle, honest.

Wherever you live, whatever happens, don't let anyone take that from you. It's the best thing about you.

chapter five

Aba.

Look closely at this one. You're still in Momi's belly. For seven years I prayed for a little brother, and in this picture you're almost here. You were literally my prayers being answered.

And look who's standing there in a CANADA shirt: me. Weird, right?

Maybe the universe was already leaving us hints. Even before you were born, a little bit of us was already in Canada waiting for you. You were never going there alone.

And here he is, holding you up. I wish he could see you now: the man, the student, the brother, the son.

Aba would be so proud of you. I know it the way I know my own name.

chapter six

The reason Momi smiles.

For years you slept beside her so she would never feel alone. You became her confidant. Her person.

I'm not exaggerating when I say you're a big reason Momi is still alive and breathing. And you're still the only one who can really make her smile.

She wrote you something. 💌

chapter seven

You still pick up.

I know there are days you don't want to talk. Days where everything feels heavy and far away.

You pick up anyway. Every time we call to check on you, you answer.

That's how understanding you are. And it means more to us than you'll ever know. 📞💛

chapter eight

I miss you.

Every time I come home, I almost call your name. Then I remember you're not there, and my heart breaks a little, every single time.

You are the best thing in my life. You always have been.

I need you to understand this, Yabu:

I could lose everything I have and live with it. Everything, except you.

chapter nine

Where we're going.

I know you don't love where you are right now. I know this year has been hard, and that being away on your birthday hurts.

But this is a chapter, not the whole book.

One day we're going to be old, rich, successful and annoying. Our kids will be running around and making noise, and we'll be laughing about the year you turned 20 in Canada and thought it was the hardest thing in the world.

And you'll look back and see that it's where you got strong.

I'm sure of it. I'm sure of you.
